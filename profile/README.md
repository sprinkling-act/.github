# Sprinkling Act

Independent pre-conformity layer for the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689).

We classify AI systems against the regulation *before* the lawyer, the auditor, or the regulator asks. Article-mapped. Dated. Defensible.

Not advisory. Not consulting. First in the chain.

## Research

**EU AI Act Readiness: A Structured Screening of 50 European AI Companies (April 2026)**
DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.19671329](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19671329) · Licensed CC BY 4.0

Key findings: 74% of 50 European AI companies trigger high-risk classification · 96% have no public AI Act position · 0 of 14 certified companies mapped the AI Act layer on top of existing certifications.
